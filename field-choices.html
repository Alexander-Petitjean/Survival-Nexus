How it works

Choose your ground. The weather chooses you.

You have a day pack, a phone at 18%, a lighter, one liter of water, a basic first-aid pouch, and the clothes you arrived in. Pick a region; a random number generator selects the season. Every option changes your condition and the choices that follow.

Training fiction—not field instructions.

These are fictional composites inspired by realistic problems, not accounts of real people. In an actual emergency, contact local emergency services and follow official guidance.

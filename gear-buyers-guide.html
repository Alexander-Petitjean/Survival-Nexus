Use this four-step process

  1. 1
    Learn

    Read the related guide and identify the actual hazard, user, environment, and failure consequences.

  2. 2
    Compare

    Check manufacturer-documented performance, maintenance, consumables, dimensions, weight, and exclusions.

  3. 3
    Shortlist

    Confirm fit, dexterity, accessibility, training, local rules, and whether a simpler option works.

  4. 4
    Verify

    Before ordering, confirm the exact model, seller, specifications, price, availability, warranty, and return policy.

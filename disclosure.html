Affiliate Disclosure

We’re transparent about how we fund Survival Nexus and the products we recommend.

1. How Affiliate Links Work

Some links on Survival Nexus are paid Amazon affiliate links. If you click one and complete a qualifying purchase, we may receive a commission. Using one of these links does not add a separate charge to your order. These earnings help maintain the site and support continued research.

2. Amazon Associates Disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Amazon may pay a percentage of qualifying revenue when a reader follows a tagged product link and completes a qualifying purchase. The percentage depends on Amazon’s classification of the purchased product, not the label Survival Nexus gives it.

Amazon controls eligibility, attribution, product categories, commission rates, cancellations, returns, and disqualifications. Its schedules can change. For the controlling current terms, see Amazon’s Associates Program Commission Income Statement.

4. Other Affiliate and Referral Programs

No other affiliate-network links are currently placed on the site. Any future material relationship will be disclosed in plain language near the relevant link before it is published.

5. Editorial Independence

  • We do not accept payment for positive reviews or placements.
  • Gear shortlists consider published specifications, manufacturer guidance, safety, reliability, and value.
  • We distinguish researched selections from items that have been personally tested.
  • Affiliate status does not determine our conclusions.

6. Identifying Paid Links

A disclosure appears close to each commercial recommendation, before or beside the link. Buttons use wording such as “paid link,” and the nearby explanation says that a qualifying purchase may compensate Survival Nexus. A general disclosure elsewhere on the site does not replace that link-level notice.

Example disclosure: This is a paid link. If you complete a qualifying purchase, I may receive compensation at no added cost to you.

If you have questions about our affiliate relationships or want a non-affiliate link to a product, contact us via our Contact Page.

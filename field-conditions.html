Recent Nearby Earthquakes

USGS-recorded earthquakes of magnitude 2.5 or greater within 500 km during the past seven days, limited to the ten most recent. This is an event catalog—not earthquake prediction or an all-clear.

Recent FEMA administrative context

The six most recently declared, unique disasters for the state resolved by NWS. Declarations are administrative records—not live warnings, current incident boundaries, or confirmation that a person qualifies for assistance.

About this data

Results come directly from National Weather Service, U.S. Geological Survey, and OpenFEMA APIs and are not cached or modified by Survival Nexus. AirNow opens as an official external map. Coverage is primarily for the United States and its territories. Times appear in your device’s local time zone.
